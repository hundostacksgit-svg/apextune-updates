Your account

Sign in

An account is only for moving a paid licence between your devices and syncing projects if you want that. The editor itself never asks you to sign in.

🔐

How your password is stored

It isn't. Your password is stretched with PBKDF2-SHA-256 at 310,000 rounds against a random 32-byte salt, and only a verifier derived from the result is kept. The password itself is never written to disk and never sent anywhere.

📟

Devices are listed, not tracked

Each device gets a random ID generated on the device. We record the browser and OS family so you can tell your laptop from your phone in the list — nothing that identifies you, and nothing shared with anyone.

🗑

Leaving is one click

Delete your account and everything goes with it, immediately. Your projects live on your device, so they stay exactly where they are — deleting the account never touches your work.
