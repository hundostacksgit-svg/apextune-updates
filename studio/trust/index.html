Written for the sceptical

Is this a scam?

No. But you have no reason to take my word for it, and "trust me" is what a scam says too. So here is what a scam would need to do that this cannot, four things you can check yourself in about a minute without giving me an email address or a card, and the full list of what this editor cannot do — which is the part nobody selling you something fake ever writes down.

Open the editor — nothing to sign up for What it can't do

Check it yourself, in a minute

Every one of these is something you do on your own machine. None of them require you to give me anything, and none of them can be faked by a website.

Open the editor. Notice what it doesn't ask for.

No account. No email. No card. No download, no extension, no "start your free trial". The link goes straight into a working editor with a timeline in it. A product that wanted your card before it showed you anything would have asked by now.

Try it: omnidx.net/studio/app

Turn your wifi off, then keep editing.

Import a clip, cut it, add an effect, export it — with no connection at all. It keeps working, because the editing happens in your browser on your own machine. This is the test that settles the privacy question properly: software that was quietly uploading your footage could not survive it.

Proves: your footage is not going anywhere

Export something and look at the file.

It is an ordinary MP4. Open it in any player, drop it into any other editor, upload it anywhere. No watermark on any tier, including the free one. No "exported with" tag, no logo burned into the corner, nothing to pay to remove later.

Proves: the free tier is the actual product, not a demo

Open a saved project in a text editor.

A project file is readable JSON — your clips, your cuts, your settings, in plain text you can read and keep. Nothing about your work is locked inside a format only this app can open, so nothing about your work depends on this app still existing.

Proves: you can leave, and take your work with you

What a scam would have to do

The patterns are well known, because they are the same every time. Here is each one, and what this does instead.

💳

Take the money first

Nothing here asks for payment before you have used the thing. The free version is the full editor with no watermark and no time limit, and most people never need to pay at all. Paying unlocks named features that are listed line by line on the pricing page before you spend anything.

🔁

Bill you again quietly

There is no subscription to forget about, because there is no subscription at all — not hidden, not "optional", not after a year. There is nothing to cancel, no card kept on file by me, and no renewal date. One payment, and that is the end of it.

🏦

Take payment somewhere with no recourse

Checkout runs through Square, a real payment processor. You pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal or Klarna, your card details never touch me, and you keep every protection your bank gives you — including the ability to go around me entirely and charge it back if I let you down.

↩️

Make refunds hard

Fourteen days, no questions, no explanation required and no retention script. Email and it gets refunded. I would genuinely rather have the honest review than the $19.99 — at this size, one person telling people it was a rip-off costs far more than a refund does.

📈

Invent the numbers

There are no testimonials on this site, no "trusted by 10,000 creators", no five-star badges and no press logos, because I do not have any of those things yet and inventing them is the cheapest thing in the world to do. The rating block in the footer shows a real average or nothing at all — it has no placeholder.

⏳

Rush you

No countdown timer, no "3 people are viewing this", no fake discount off a price that was never charged, no launch-week-only pricing. $19.99 is the price. If you come back in six months and it is cheaper, that is your good luck, not a trick played on you now.

Everything it can't do

This is the honest list, and it is the reason to believe the rest of the site. Anyone can write a features page. Almost nobody writes this one.

Generate video that isn't there It cannot swap an object for a different object, invent scenery, or extend a shot past its edges. That needs a generative video model, which cannot run in a browser — so anything offering it is either lying or sending your footage to somebody else's server. It can remove an object and fill the space from the real background, because that is solvable honestly.
Write your captions for you, yet Right now captions find the speech and lay out correctly-timed boxes, and you type the words. Turning audio into text needs a transcription server, which is not switched on yet. When it is, the same button transcribes. Buy on what it does today, not on that.
Stabilise a shaky shot Not built. A stabiliser that only counters side-to-side movement makes rolling-shutter wobble worse rather than better, and doing it properly means solving rotation and scale too. Left out rather than shipped as something that helps some shots and ruins others.
Draw a mask by hand with a pen Masks come as rectangles, ovals and polygons — feathered, rotatable, animatable and trackable. There is no bezier pen for tracing an arbitrary outline freehand.
Guarantee a licence can't be shared Entitlement checks run on your own machine, and anyone determined enough can switch them off with browser dev tools. I would rather say that than pretend at a lock that does not exist. The honest deal is that paying is how this keeps being made.
Sync your projects between devices, yet Your licence works on every device you own, but projects live on the device you made them on until the account server is switched on. You can move a project by exporting the file and opening it on the other machine.
Beat a $6,000 workstation at 8K It is a browser. Long 4K timelines on an old laptop will be slower than DaVinci Resolve on a machine built for it. Playback drops preview resolution to stay smooth and exports at full quality, which is the right trade, but it is a trade.

If something on this list matters to you, use the free version and find out before you pay, or don't pay. That is the whole point of there being a free version.

Where your footage goes

Nowhere. Here is the precise version of that, including the exceptions.

🖥️

The editing is on your machine

Importing, cutting, effects, colour, audio and export all run in your browser. Your clips are held in your browser's own storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to be edited, which is why the whole thing works with the network off.

☁️

The three exceptions, named

Two features can talk to a server when they are switched on: the cloud AI planner, and caption transcription. Both send only what they need, both say so at the time, and both are off unless configured. The third is smaller and worth naming anyway: picking one of the web typefaces for a title fetches that font file from Google Fonts, once. No footage is involved in any of the three, and with no connection the font simply falls back.

🚫

No analytics on your edit

There is no tracker watching what you cut, no telemetry on your timeline, and the editor's page loads no third-party script at all — you can read that off the network panel in ten seconds. What you make is not data I collect.

🔌

Test it, don't trust it

Aeroplane mode, then edit. Or open your browser's network panel and watch it stay quiet while you work — the only thing you should ever see go out is a font file, if you picked one. A claim you can check in ten seconds is worth more than a paragraph of promises, and this one is checkable.

What happens if I disappear

The fair question about a one-time purchase from somebody you have never heard of. A subscription dies with the company. This mostly doesn't.

📴

The app keeps running

Install it and the whole editor is cached on your device. It opens and works with the site gone and the network off, because it was never running on my server in the first place.

🗂️

Your projects stay readable

Plain JSON on your machine. Even with no app at all, the file tells you what your edit was — and your exports are ordinary MP4s that were never tied to anything.

🖥️

The desktop build is yours

The Mac and Windows downloads are a normal application sitting on your own drive. Nobody can reach in and switch it off, and it does not phone home to check it is allowed to start.

Who is behind this

One person, building this on their own. Not a company, not a team, and I am not going to pretend otherwise with a fake "About us" page and stock photographs of an office. That is the honest weakness of buying from me: there is no support department behind this, and if I am asleep your email waits until morning.

It is also the honest strength. Nobody above me needs a growth number next quarter, which is the reason there is no subscription, no watermark and no upsell in the middle of your edit — those things exist to hit targets I do not have.
