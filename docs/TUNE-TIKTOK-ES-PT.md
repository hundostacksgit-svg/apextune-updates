The same fourteen videos as `docs/TUNE-TIKTOK.md`, with the caption, hashtags and
pinned comment written for Spanish-speaking (ES) and Brazilian (PT) viewers. The
text on screen stays in English; the caption and the comment carry the language.
Point both at the page in their language: **omnidx.net/studio/es/** and
**omnidx.net/studio/pt/** (the front page, translated, built by
`tools/build-lang.py`).

The house rules hold in every language: no frame-rate figure, no "X% más
rápido / mais rápido", no competitor named, no "IA", and the example PC's numbers
called an example (**PC de ejemplo** / **PC de exemplo**).

A second account per language posts best (TikTok shows a video to people who
speak the language of the caption and the account). Post in the evening of their
time zone: Mexico City / Bogotá for ES, São Paulo for PT.
