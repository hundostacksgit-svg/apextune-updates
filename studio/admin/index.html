Owner

Support from a phone: look an order up by its Square reference or by a key, send the keys again, switch an order off or back on, free a key from its PC. Nothing here works without the owner token, and switching keys off or freeing them takes two presses.

Remembered in this browser only.

A refund in Square switches an order off by itself; "Switch the order off" is for the rest, and "Switch off this key only" is for a partial refund of a Squad order (type the key). "Free this key" takes the key you typed (or the first of the order) off its PC without spending the buyer's own monthly move. "Send a test email" proves the key emails deliver before the first sale: it goes to the support address (or the one typed above), and when Resend refuses, its exact reason shows here. "Send every unsent order" is for the day mail was off or refused while orders came in: every order marked "not emailed" goes out once, to its checkout address. "Email me the week" sends the figures (last seven days and since the first sale) to the support address now; the same email arrives by itself every Monday morning.
