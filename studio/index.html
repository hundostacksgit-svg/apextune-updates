Network

Latency over throughput on the adapter you actually use.

Downloads are not made slower — the "disable autotuning" myth that halves your speed is left where it belongs. What changes is how fast a small packet leaves your PC and how often the adapter is allowed to nap.

📶

On the live adapter

  • Nagle's algorithm off: small packets go now, not after a 200 ms wait for company
  • Adapter power management off, so the link never drops to save a watt
  • Energy-efficient Ethernet, green Ethernet, interrupt moderation, ultra-low-power mode off — by whichever name your driver uses
  • Optionally, DNS to 1.1.1.1 with -Dns. Off by default; your router is usually fine

In the TCP stack

  • The 20% bandwidth reservation for Windows' own traffic set to zero
