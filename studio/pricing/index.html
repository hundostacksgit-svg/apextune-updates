There is no subscription. On purpose.

We took the monthly option off the table entirely. A subscription is a bill that arrives whether you edited anything that month or not, and chasing expired cards is a job we'd rather not have. Buy it once, own it, and if we ever go away the last version you have keeps working — it runs on your machine, not ours.

14 days, no questions

Ask for a refund within two weeks and you get one. You don't have to explain yourself and you don't have to talk to anybody — there's a button in your account. We'd rather have the honest review than the $39.99.

📵

No upsells inside the editor

Paid features are labelled once, in grey, where they live. There are no popups, no countdown banners and no "unlock now" interruptions in the middle of a cut. Everything you can click, you can use.

Students and under-18s

Email us from a school address and Creator is free for a year. We're not going to make a teenager choose between an editor and lunch.

Questions people actually ask

Is the free version watermarked?

No. Not on export, not in the corner, not anywhere. A watermark on someone's work to advertise our software is a bad trade for both of us.

Is there a subscription option?

No, and there won't be. Every edition is one payment. Nothing renews, nothing expires, and your licence does not stop working if you never give us another penny. Future updates to the edition you bought are included.

Can I use the AI on the free version?

No — the AI editor starts at Creator. That is the honest answer, so here is the honest reason: every AI request costs us real money to run, and a free tier that spends it is a free tier that eventually gets switched off. Everything that runs on your own device stays free forever: the timeline, all 20 free filters, the 11 one-tap edit styles, the audio filters, stickers, transitions, proxies and 1080p export with no watermark. The one-tap styles in particular build a full edit for you without any AI at all.

What is the Team edition?

$69.99 once, for exactly three people — everything in Studio, a shared licence and shared projects. Three separate Studio licences would be $119.97, so it saves a real $49.98. Three means three: the seat count is checked on our server when someone joins, not politely suggested in the app.

Does one purchase cover my laptop and my phone?

Yes. Creator covers 3 devices, Studio covers 10. Sign in on each one. You can swap a device out any time from your account page.

Can I pay with Cash App?

Yes — Cash App Pay is one of the buttons at checkout, alongside card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and Klarna. Pick whichever you like; it all ends up in the same place.

Do I need an account to use the editor?

No. Open it and start cutting. An account exists for two reasons only: to move a paid licence between your devices, and to sync projects if you turn that on. The free editor never asks.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere. Editing and export happen on your device. The only thing that ever leaves is an AI request, and only if you use the AI assistant with a cloud key configured — the built-in planner runs locally.

Is this a real editor or a template app?

A real editor. Multitrack timeline, frame-accurate trimming, keyframes, colour tools, audio mixing, export presets. The AI is a layer on top of that, not a substitute for it.

What if I bought Creator and want Studio later?

You pay the difference, not the full price. It's a button in your account.

How do I get access after paying?

You already have it. Paying unlocks the app on the spot — Square sends you straight back to us and your copy is unlocked before you have finished reading the receipt. There is no licence key, no email to wait for, and nothing to type in.

If something goes wrong on the way back — you lost signal, or closed the tab — go to Unlock my copy, pick what you bought, and you are in. Your Square receipt is proof enough; you do not need anything else.

How do I use it on my other devices?

Make an account on the account page and the licence follows it. Creator covers 3 devices, Studio 10, Team 3 people. Until you do, the copy you paid on stays unlocked on its own — an account is for moving it, not for using it.

Will the price go up?

Maybe, for new buyers. Never for you — a one-time purchase includes every future update at no extra cost, and that's not something we're going to walk back later.
