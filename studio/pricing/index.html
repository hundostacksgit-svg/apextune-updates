Questions people actually ask

Is the free version watermarked?

No. Not on export, not in the corner, not anywhere. A watermark on someone's work to advertise our software is a bad trade for both of us.

What happens to my projects if I stop paying the monthly plan?

They open, they play, they export. You drop to the Starter feature set, so an AI-assisted edit stays exactly as it is on the timeline — you just can't run new AI actions until you upgrade again. Nothing is deleted and nothing is held hostage.

Does one purchase cover my laptop and my phone?

Yes. Creator covers 3 devices, Studio covers 10. Sign in on each one. You can swap a device out any time from your account page.

Can I pay with Cash App?

Yes — Cash App Pay is one of the buttons at checkout, alongside card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and Klarna. Pick whichever you like; it all ends up in the same place.

Do I need an account to use the editor?

No. Open it and start cutting. An account exists for two reasons only: to move a paid licence between your devices, and to sync projects if you turn that on. The free editor never asks.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere. Editing and export happen on your device. The only thing that ever leaves is an AI request, and only if you use the AI assistant with a cloud key configured — the built-in planner runs locally.

Is this a real editor or a template app?

A real editor. Multitrack timeline, frame-accurate trimming, keyframes, colour tools, audio mixing, export presets. The AI is a layer on top of that, not a substitute for it.

What if I bought Creator and want Studio later?

You pay the difference, not the full price. It's a button in your account.

Will the price go up?

Maybe, for new buyers. Never for you — a one-time purchase includes every future update at no extra cost, and that's not something we're going to walk back later.

Try the free one first.

It's the whole editor. If it doesn't do what you need, don't give us any money.
