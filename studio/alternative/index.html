Looking for a CapCut alternative, or an editor with no subscription?

A video editor you buy once.
Or don't buy at all.

OmniDx Studio runs in the browser and on your phone. The free edition has no watermark, no export cap and no account. The paid editions are a single payment — $19.99 for Creator, $39.99 for Studio — and there is no monthly option, on purpose.

Open the editor See every price

What "no subscription" means here

Nothing renews

You pay once and the edition is yours. No card on file, no expiry, nothing that switches off in a year.

Free is complete

The timeline, twenty filters, the one-tap edit styles, transitions, stickers, audio filters and 1080p export — no watermark, no cap, no clock.

Nothing leaves your device

Editing, effects, export and the music library run on your machine. The trust page lists exactly what is and isn't on a server.

Coming from another editor

Naming them is fair; they are what most people use. This is what changes when you move.

If you useWhat's different here
CapCutBeat-cut edits, templates and effects are here too — plus scopes, ProRes export on desktop, multicam, audio repair and expressions, and it works with no account and no connection.
Premiere Pro or After EffectsKeyframes with bezier easing, motion tracking, masks, shape layers with trim paths, per-character text animators, parenting and nulls — for one payment rather than a monthly one.
DaVinci ResolveThree-way wheels, curves, LUTs, an HSL qualifier, power windows, waveform, vectorscope and RGB parade, in a browser tab.
iMovie or a phone appThe same editor on the phone and the desktop, the same project on both, and 4K60 export from either.

Every row on this table is something in the build you can open right now. Anything still being built is marked so on the pricing page.

What it can't do yet

Two things people ask for that are not here today: speech-to-text captions (the app finds the speech timings; you type the words) and stabilisation. Both are on the trust page so nobody finds out after paying.

Try it before you decide anything

It opens in a tab. No account, no download, no watermark on what you export.
