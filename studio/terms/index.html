Terms, privacy and money

One page, in plain words, because a thing that asks for admin rights and a card number owes you that. If anything here and the rest of the site disagree, this page wins.

What you are buying

A key, not a program. The script is public; the key is what makes it run on your PC.

🔑

One key, one PC

OmniDx Tune is $19.99, paid once, for a key that runs the tune on one PC for as long as you own it: as many times as you like, after every Windows update, free. Squad is $39.99, paid once, for three keys, one PC each: one for you, two to give away. A key locks to the first PC that runs it; a second PC on the same key is refused.

🙅

What a key is not

Not for resale, not for a shop of "PC optimisation" clients, not for sharing beyond the PCs it covers. A key from an order that Square refunds or charges back stops working; the settings it made stay until you undo them. The script itself is readable by anyone, on purpose, and reading it needs no key.

🖥️

Where it runs

Windows 10 and Windows 11, on a PC you own or are allowed to administer. Not a work laptop somebody else manages, not a school machine, not a PC where a game's or a tournament's rules forbid changing system settings. It changes Windows' own settings and, when you say yes, the graphics lines in a supported game's own settings file (backed up first); never a game's program files or its anti-cheat. The rules you play under are yours to keep.

🦅

OmniDx Edition comes with it

Every key includes OmniDx Edition at no extra charge: apps, pictures and settings that set up genuine Windows for games, on a clean install or on the PC as it is, each value written down so one command puts it back. It is not a copy of Windows and does not include a Windows licence: Windows comes from Microsoft, under Microsoft's licence, which stays yours to hold. Windows is a trademark of Microsoft; OmniDx is not Microsoft and is not endorsed by it.

🧑

Who you are dealing with

OmniDx is one person, named on the trust page, selling through Square. The support address is at the bottom of every page and in the key email; replies come from the same person who wrote the script.

Money: paid once, and every sale is final

Paid once through Square. Nothing renews. There is no account to close. There is no refund, and here is why that is fair.

💳

Paying

Square takes the payment; the card details go to Square and never to us. The moment Square confirms the payment the key is issued and shown on the page Square sends you to; when emailing is switched on it also goes to the email you typed at checkout. Prices are in US dollars; your bank sets any conversion.

🔒

All sales are final

The key is delivered on the page the moment you pay and it is yours for as long as you own the PC, so there is nothing to send back and no refund for changing your mind. Look before you buy: the free report mode runs the same script on your PC, lists every change it would make and makes none of them, with no key and nothing to pay. A refund or chargeback that reaches Square anyway switches the order's keys off; a tuned PC keeps its settings and its undo either way.

📦

If something is wrong

Write to the support address. A key that will not take on your PC is fixed, moved or reissued until it works; a PC that the tune broke and undo did not put right is helped the same day, with the undo file and the run's log. What is fixed is the key and the PC, not the price: "I got fewer frames than I hoped" is a conversation, and usually the BIOS checklist, because frame rates are never promised.

🔁

A new PC

A key moves to a new PC from the key page by yourself, with your order or receipt number, at a sensible interval; more often than that is a support email, answered the same day. A new board or a fresh Windows on the same board counts as the same PC.

What leaves your PC, and where it is kept

The full list. There is no analytics script on this site and no tracking cookie.

🧾

From the purchase

Square passes us the order id, the short receipt number, the email address you typed at checkout and the amount. That is how the key is issued, found again from your receipt, and emailed when emailing is on. We never see the card.

🖥️

From the key check

When the script runs it sends the key and a fingerprint of the PC: a hash of the board serial, the system id and the CPU id, which identifies the machine without describing it. With it go the CPU and GPU names, the Windows build and the script version, so your own machines can be told apart on the key page. Nothing else leaves the PC: the report, the logs, the backups and the undo stay in C:\OmniDx.

🌐

From the site

Your key, once issued, is remembered in your own browser so the key page can show it again, and so is the light-or-dark choice; both are on your device, not ours. The star rating at the foot of the page, if you use it, sends the stars, your note and a random id for the device so one device counts once. No analytics, no advertising pixels, no cookies from anyone else.

🗄️

Where it lives, for how long

The licence table (keys, orders, the checkout email, the machine fingerprints) is a Cloudflare database; the site is on GitHub Pages; when emailing is switched on, the key emails go through the owner's Gmail or Resend. The licence row is kept for as long as the key is meant to work, which is as long as you own the PC. Ask and it is deleted, which switches the key off. Support emails are kept while they are needed to help you and then no longer.

Your rights, without the form: ask what is held against your order or key and it is sent to you; ask for it to be deleted and it is; ask for the key to be moved or the email on file changed and it is. The support address does all three.

What it does to your PC, and what we promise

It changes settings. That is the product. Here is the shape of the responsibility.

🛠️

What it changes

Startup apps, services, scheduled tasks, preinstalled apps, telemetry switches, the power plan, network and scheduler settings, visual effects, per-game profiles and, when you say yes, the graphics lines in supported games' own settings files, every one listed with its reason on the what-it-touches page. It runs as administrator because those settings need it. It makes a System Restore point and an undo file first, and it never touches Defender, the firewall, Secure Boot, your drivers or your files.

🤝

What we promise

That the script does what this site says and nothing more; that every change has an undo; that the copy your command fetches is the exact one that passed the published checks; that a key you paid for is made to work on your PC, moved or reissued by support until it does. Frame rates are not promised: they depend on the game, the hardware and the BIOS, and the report is honest about that.

📋

What is yours

Backing up what matters before changing a PC, reading the report, doing the BIOS checklist or not, and deciding whether Extreme is for you. The tune asks before anything that costs a convenience, and undo puts it all back, but you are the administrator of your own machine.
