# The business: edits for local pages, on a monthly retainer

Written the day the owner said "I need something I can make into a business,
I don't know how to get a following, my videos don't appear on anyone's FYP."

## Why the FYP is not the problem to solve

TikTok tests every upload on a hundred to three hundred people and only pushes
it further if they watch. A promo for an app nobody has heard of gets skipped
in the first second, so every test fails, so every video stops at a hundred
views. That is the mechanism. It is not a ban, and better promos do not fix it,
because the thing being skipped is "an ad for something I don't know."

So the business cannot depend on the FYP. This one does not.

## The business

**Beat-cut short-form edits for local businesses, $50 each or $150 a month for
four.** Car dealers and detailers, gyms and fight gyms, barbers, restaurants,
sneaker and clothing resellers. They already pay for social content, they
cannot make it themselves, and they do not care about follower counts — they
care whether the edit on the phone in front of them looks good.

Sold two ways, neither of which needs an audience:

1. **In person.** Walk in, show the rate card on the phone, offer the first one
   free. Ten shops a day on any main street in NJ.
2. **By DM**, to pages under 20k followers that post phone footage, with the
   same free first one. The script is in `SELL-EDITS.md`.

Every delivered edit carries `omnidx.net` in the corner on the client's page to
the client's followers. The app gets its marketing from work that pays.

**The rate card**, built to be held up on a phone at a counter:
https://claude.ai/artifact/Kgg5GdhwX6W9WAzjQH4D2h

## The maths, honestly

| | |
|---|---|
| One shop on the monthly | $150/mo |
| Five shops on the monthly | $750/mo |
| Ten shops on the monthly | $1,500/mo |
| Time per edit with the pipeline | 20–40 minutes |
| Time per week at ten shops | ~15 hours |

Ten retainers is a real month's money for fifteen hours a week, and it is
reachable in four to six weeks from zero if the free first one goes out every
day. Nobody signs a retainer on a pitch. They sign after the free one is on
their page and their followers reacted to it.

## The pitch, in person (twenty seconds)

> Hey — I make the short edits for pages like yours, the ones cut to the beat.
> I did a few for [type of business] round here. I'll do one for you free
> tonight so you can see it — I just need ten seconds of phone footage of
> [the car / the cut / the room]. If you like it, they're fifty each or one a
> week for a hundred and fifty a month.

Then hand them the phone with the rate card up. Do not explain the app. If
they ask what you edit with, say the name and move on.

The free one is not a discount, it is the whole sales process. It costs
twenty minutes and it turns "some kid" into "the guy who did our video."

## This weekend: the car meet

Car pages are the audience the mog references come from, and NJ has a meet
somewhere every Saturday and Sunday. Go to one.

- Film ten cars with the phone — ten seconds each, low angle, one slow pass.
- Ask each owner for their handle. Say you will tag them.
- Edit five that night on the reference cuts, post them tagged, DM the owners
  the file.
- Charge nothing for those. Charge $20 a car from the next meet on, and have
  the rate card up on the phone at the meet.

Car owners repost anything that makes their car look good. Five reposts to
five car-page audiences is more distribution in one night than the account
has had in a month, and every one of them carries the sticker.

## The week

| Day | |
|---|---|
| Mon | Ten shops in person on one street. Rate card up. Offer the free one. |
| Tue | Deliver Monday's free ones by 10pm. Twenty DMs. |
| Wed | Ten shops on a second street. |
| Thu | Deliver. Follow up Monday's: "did it do anything for you? want the monthly?" |
| Fri | Twenty DMs. Deliver. |
| Sat | Car meet. Film ten, edit five, post tagged. |
| Sun | Deliver the rest. Count: shops visited, free ones delivered, paid, retainers. |

Fifty shops a week. If one in ten takes the free one and one in three of those
takes the monthly, that is roughly two retainers a week. Slow the first week,
because nobody has heard of you. Faster the third, because three shops on one
street have.

## What not to do

- Do not spend the week making more promos for the app. They will get a
  hundred views each, and the week is gone.
- Do not lead with the app. Lead with the edit on the phone.
- Do not price the free one. It is free. The retainer is where the money is.
